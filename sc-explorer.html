Supply Chain Explorer

Interactive explorer for company-level supply chain networks

I designed and built this tool for a supply chain network research project with Prof. Deepak Agrawal. It maps roughly 36,000 companies and 3.3 million quarterly supplier–customer links from Bloomberg SPLC and FactSet data. Click any company card to inspect it; shift-click to make it the focal company. It is also featured on Prof. Agrawal's website.
